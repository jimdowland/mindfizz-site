Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Mindfizz Ltd uses personal information submitted through this website.

Who we are

Mindfizz Ltd is the controller of the personal information covered by this notice. The company is registered in England and Wales under company number 5842619. You can contact us at studio@mindfizz.com.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organisation and details of your enquiry. Our hosting provider may also record standard technical information in server logs for security and reliability.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirements, prepare proposals and manage any subsequent working relationship. We do not add contact-form details to a marketing list.

Our lawful basis

We process enquiries because it is in our legitimate interests to respond to people who contact our business and, where applicable, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

We may use trusted service providers for website hosting and email. They may process information only as needed to provide those services. We do not sell personal information. If a provider processes information outside the UK, we require an appropriate legal safeguard.

How long we keep it

We normally retain enquiry correspondence for up to two years so that we can follow up and maintain an appropriate business record. If an enquiry becomes a client project, relevant records may be retained for longer where required for contractual, tax or legal purposes.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your information, restrict its use, or object to processing. To exercise a right, email studio@mindfizz.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Updates

We may update this notice when our services or legal obligations change. This version was last updated on 13 July 2026.
