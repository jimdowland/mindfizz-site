Who we are

Twenty-five years of turning complex material into communication that works.

Mindfizz is a small communication design studio with 25 years' experience helping organisations turn unclear or complex material into structured presentations, reports and visual resources.

Our role is often to help teams clarify what needs to be communicated. We then shape the material into communication that can be easily understood.

Examples of how we have helped

A selection of projects where we were brought in early to shape the structure and narrative.

Charity / Third sector
Board-level transformation plan

Turned fragmented consultant notes into an evidence based, structured review and transformation plan, enabling the board to assess whether moving from a relationship management model to a stakeholder management approach was the right strategic direction for their enterprise division.

Energy / Technical
Technical pitch for a global energy audience

Restructured a highly technical presentation on legacy seismic data analysis into a clear value story, helping senior leaders understand the benefits while retaining sufficient technical detail for specialist audiences.

Legal / Professional services
Transformation presentation
