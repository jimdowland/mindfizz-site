Organisations invest significant resources in research, evidence and reports. But many people, who may benefit from reading the report, never come into contact with the key findings, or read the document.

## The completed report isn’t the end of the process

If we think of a workforce strategy report as an example. A workforce strategy report is a document that looks at an industry’s current labour supply and how it meets future demands.

The document covers;

- an examination of the current workforce including demographics, skills and turnover.
- projections of roles and skills the industry will need in the future.
- Analysis of skills gaps
- Strategies for addressing any identified skills gaps

## From reports to social media

To reach a wider audience a social media campaign can be built around the findings of the report. For this to be successful we will look at the following.

### Audience

## Finding the messages within the evidence

The purpose of the campaign is different from the actual report, while the report presents evidence and offers a considered assessment, the social media tiles perform more like a blurb on the back of a book. The capture attention and stimulate interest. In our selection we’ll look for material that could work independently of the report. Striking statistics, emerging risks, recommendations and leadership perspectives.

## Illustrative reconstructions

- Progress – women’s representation in tech roles at two points in time. Using charts and counters
- Risk – the retirement cliff edge- Visualise how an aging workforce could effect capacity
- Perspectives – an animated quote or a short talking head video
- Opportunity – Future careers. A message directed to people considering careers in the sector.
